Privacy Policy

Shareff Browser Extension

Last updated: February 5, 2026

Shareff is a simple browser extension for sharing links between small groups of friends. It is not a social network, does not serve ads, and does not track you. This policy explains exactly what data is involved and how it's handled.

What Shareff Stores

On your device (via browser extension storage):

  • Your name and email address (entered by you in Settings)
  • The email address of friends you've connected with
  • A list of link IDs you've already read (so we can show unread badges)

On our server:

  • Your name and email address
  • The names and email addresses of friends in your network
  • Links shared within your network (URL, page title, sender, recipient, timestamp)
  • Group names and membership (if you create groups)

Each network's data is stored as a single flat text file on the server. There is no database, no analytics platform, and no third-party data storage.

What Shareff Does NOT Collect

  • Browsing history
  • Cookies or tracking identifiers
  • Location data
  • Device information or fingerprints
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Any data from pages you visit (unless you explicitly share a link)

How Your Data Is Used

Your data is used for one purpose: delivering shared links between you and your friends. Specifically:

  • Your email identifies you within your friend group
  • Your name is shown to friends when you send them a link
  • Shared links are stored so recipients can retrieve them
  • Email addresses are used to send invite emails when you add a new friend

That's it. Your data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or analyzed in any way.

To delete your network, the extension just deletes your text file on the server.

Network Communication

The extension communicates only with our server (dbooth.net/shareff/) over HTTPS. It makes requests to:

  • Register your name and email
  • Send and retrieve shared links
  • Manage your friend connections and groups
  • Send invite emails to new friends

The extension does not contact any other servers or services.

Permissions Explained

  • storage — Saves your settings and read-link history locally in your browser
  • contextMenus — Adds "Shareff" to the right-click menu so you can send links
  • notifications — Shows a browser notification when someone sends you a link
  • alarms — Periodically checks for new links (every 20 minutes)
  • activeTab — Gets the URL and title of the current page when you choose to share it
  • scripting — Shows a brief toast message on the page confirming a link was sent

Data Retention & Deletion

Links are retained on the server up to a maximum of 100 per network. Older links are automatically removed as new ones are added.

You can delete all your data at any time:

  • Server data: Open Settings and click "Delete Network" to permanently delete the text file that contains all friend, group, and link info from the server
  • Local data: Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored settings

Who Can See Your Links

The goal is for the group admin to be able to send a link to a friend and that friend can send a link to the admin, Friends in the same cannot send links to other friends in the network.

A Group just loops over a list of friends and send a link. To the friend in the group, it looks the same. Friends don't know they are in a group.
