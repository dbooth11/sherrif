How Shareff Works

Shareff is a browser extension for sharing links between friends. There's no app to install, no account to create, and no social feed to scroll — just a right-click menu and a notification when someone sends you something.

When you find something worth sharing, right-click the page and select your friend's name from the Shareff menu. They'll get a browser notification and the link will appear in their extension popup.

Two Roles, One Extension

Shareff has two roles that work together. Most people start as a recipient (the person who was invited) but anyone can be both.

Admin (Sender)

  • Invites friends by email
  • Creates groups (e.g. "Science", "Sports")
  • Sends links to individuals or groups
  • Receives links back from friends

Recipient

  • Connects to an admin via their email
  • Receives links from any group they're in
  • Sends links back to the admin
  • Can also be an admin for their own friends
Two-way sharing: Sharing isn't one-directional. Once you're connected, you can right-click any page and send it back to your friend. They'll see it in their inbox just like you see theirs.

Groups

Admins can create groups to organize who receives which links. For example, an admin might have a "Science" group and a "Sports" group with different people in each. When they send a link to "Science", only the people in that group see it.

As a recipient, you don't need to worry about groups — you'll just see all the links from any group you belong to in one feed.

Your Links Feed

Click the Shareff icon in your browser toolbar to see your links. New links are marked with a badge and sorted to the top. Click any link to open it and mark it as read. You can also search and archive old links.

The extension icon shows a green number when you have unread links, so you always know when something new has arrived.

Privacy

Shareff is private by design. There's no public profile, no algorithm, and no tracking. Links are only visible to the people they're sent to. The extension only communicates with our server to send and receive links — nothing else.
