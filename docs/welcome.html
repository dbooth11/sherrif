Shareff

The simplest way to share links with friends

You've been invited!
Someone wants to share links with you using Shareff. It's a simple browser extension that lets friends send each other interesting links without leaving the browser.

Getting Started

  1. Install the Extension Click the button below to add Shareff to your browser
  2. Enter Your Info Just your name and email so your friend knows it's you
  3. Connect to Your Friend Enter their email address in the "Receive Links" section
  4. Start Sharing! Right-click any page to send links back and forth
